# Fiscal sponsorship

Create a page about fiscal sponsorship on /fiscal-sponsorship to explain what it is, the conditions, and how to apply.

## Why are we offering fiscal sponsorship?
Often times, we have individuals or groups that want to organise an event at the Commons Hub, sell tickets for it, but they don't have a Stripe account to be able to sell tickets. So they ended up using our own Luma account and we would receive the money meant for their event. Then, we would keep some to pay the rent of the room, and the organisers would submit their expenses up to the amount that was left. We were effectively sharing in common not only a physical space but also a common legal entity and bank account to make it easier for everyone to not only organise events but also charge for them. Offering fiscal sponsorship is just a way to formalize that relationship.

## Who is this for?

For anyone or any collective of people that organises events at the Commons Hub Brussels.
For anyone or any collective developing digital commons in open source and working every now and then from the Commons Hub Brussels.

Examples: 
- you are organising an event or a workshop, you want to sell tickets via Luma (or any other ticketing app), but you don't have a legal entity yet for your activity or collective. E.g. Genesis https://opencollective.com/genesisxp
- you are developing an open source tool for the commons and one of your developers is working from Brussels and needs a coworking space. You'd like to receive donations or subsidies for your project. E.g. https://openletter.earth

## conditions

At least one core contributor (admin) of your collective needs to be a member of the Commons Hub Brussels.

Your collective organises at least one event per year at the Commons Hub Brussels or at least one of your core contributors regularly comes to work at the Commons Hub Brussels (at least once every other months).

You commit to transparency. All income and expenses are shared publicly in open source (without personal details).

I.e. given the legal liability, we only offer this service to people and collectives that we get to see on a regular basis. 

## Costs:

We take 10% of all income to cover admin and accounting fees. Any surplus is reinvested to improve the common space used by all projects fiscally hosted by the Commons Hub Brussels.

## FAQ

### What's the legal status of our collective?
If you are fiscally hosted by us, you are effectively legally becoming a project of our non-profit (ASBL/VZW). But since our governance is based on sociocracy, you are running your own circle and take your own decisions on how to spend the money that you receive.

### We have a sponsor for our event/project, can we send them an invoice?
Yes, that's the all reason to have a fiscal host. They will receive a proper invoice from us with VAT (if applicable). 

## Do we have to pay VAT?
If you provide a good or a service in exchange of money, we need to add VAT to the invoices you issue as a collective. 

The balance of your collective will increase with the amount without VAT (since we have to send the VAT to the state). If it's a donation (unconditional support without any service or good offered in return), there is no VAT.

### Can we get the VAT back?
No, it's too much admin for us at this stage. So if you have expenses, you file them to your collective and you'll be reimbursed. If there was VAT, we may or may not claim it back. If we do, it's an extra contribution to the commons (so you indirectly get it back).

### Can I submit any expense?
Any valid expense that is directly related to your project (public transport tickets, restaurants, consumables, consulting, etc.). A valid expense can be a photo of a receipt as long as we can clearly see the name and address of the place, the date, the item(s) purchased. It can also be a PDF (e.g. for online services). Freelancers can also issue an invoice (to be addressed to [Collective Name], Citizen Spring ASBL, rue de Villers 12, 1000 Brussels, VAT: BE0804505132).
Expenses need to be approved by at least a second person from your collective.
